# Lumora AI

## About This App

Lumora AI is an AI-powered Career and Education Intelligence platform designed to help students, university applicants, undergraduates, and fresh graduates make informed decisions about their academic and professional futures.

The platform combines career guidance, scholarship discovery, learning roadmaps, CV evaluation, portfolio development, and interview preparation into a single intelligent ecosystem. By leveraging artificial intelligence, Lumora AI provides personalized recommendations based on each user's goals, interests, skills, and academic background.

Its mission is to simplify career planning and educational development by providing practical tools, insights, and guidance that help users achieve their full potential.

---

# Problem Statement

Many students and young professionals face significant challenges when preparing for their future careers and educational opportunities. Common problems include:

* Difficulty identifying suitable career paths based on personal strengths and interests.
* Limited access to relevant and personalized scholarship opportunities.
* Lack of structured learning plans for acquiring new skills.
* Poorly optimized CVs and portfolios that fail to meet industry expectations.
* Insufficient preparation for job interviews and scholarship interviews.
* Uncertainty about their readiness for academic and professional opportunities.

These challenges often result in missed opportunities, poor decision-making, and slower personal development.

---

# Objectives

Lumora AI aims to:

* Help users discover career paths aligned with their skills and interests.
* Provide personalized scholarship recommendations.
* Generate structured learning roadmaps for skill development.
* Improve the quality of CVs and professional portfolios.
* Prepare users for interviews through AI-powered simulations.
* Increase users' confidence and readiness for educational and career opportunities.

---

# Key Features

### Career Analyzer

Analyzes user profiles and provides personalized career recommendations based on skills, interests, and educational background.

### Scholarship Finder

Identifies and recommends scholarship opportunities that match the user's qualifications and goals.

### Study Planner

Creates customized learning roadmaps with clear milestones and progress tracking.

### CV Assistant

Reviews resumes, evaluates strengths and weaknesses, and provides actionable improvement suggestions.

### Portfolio Builder

Helps users organize and showcase projects, achievements, and professional experiences effectively.

### Mock Interview

Simulates real-world interviews using AI-generated questions and feedback to improve performance.

### Opportunity Score

Measures user readiness for academic and professional opportunities through a comprehensive scoring system.

### Progress Tracking

Monitors learning consistency, completed goals, and overall development progress.
